Coraline 1.15.45 — exploration, portals and pursuit

Changes
- Activity portals use side lawns, about 20 blocks beyond the outer house footprint. Front-approach sites are rejected, including saved portal positions.
- During caught/scolding conversations Mother approaches to roughly 1–1.5 blocks and faces the player's eyes. Safe NPC repositioning handles blocked approaches; the existing four-block player conversation area remains.
- The Other World radius is now 500 blocks. Re-entering an existing completed realm expands the outer terrain while preserving the old disk, including mixed boundary chunks. Copy work remains asynchronous and budgeted.
- Structure capacity increased from 48 to 96, with a 24-site quota per tier. Generation favors underrepresented tiers and extends highest-tier sites into the expanded outer region. Actual placement still requires safe natural terrain.
- Newly generated structures have broken roofs, cobwebs, cracked/mossy masonry, rubble, dead vegetation and soul lighting. Gameplay entrances, clue nodes and ground-floor access protections remain intact.
- Other Iron, Other Diamond, Moonstone and Soulglass ore blocks generate in small surface deposits at least 60 blocks beyond the estate. Soulglass occurs only 180+ blocks out and has a 0.2% chance per eligible deposit. Persisted sampling prevents regeneration while walking back and forth.
- Normal pickaxe mining/drop processing: stone tier for Other Iron, iron tier for Other Diamond/Moonstone, diamond tier for Soulglass. Silk Touch works; Fortune applies except to Soulglass. Existing material assets are reused, with no new image/audio payload.
- Corrected shared custom-tool requirement tags that incorrectly excluded vanilla pickaxes.
- Searching/chasing Mother uses a forward 120-degree vision cone and line of sight. Seeing the player starts pursuit, not immediate capture. Capture requires bounding-box contact; after losing sight she pursues the last visible position for four seconds, then resumes search.
- Direct chases no longer use teleport recovery. Spy reports send Mother to investigate instead of teleporting her beside the player.

Installation and limits
- Replace the old mod JAR and restart Minecraft. Leave/re-enter the Other World to expand an already-created realm.
- Outer terrain and structures build gradually. A fresh visit gives the fully updated structure distribution/ruined styles; already-built structures are not demolished mid-visit.
- Safe-terrain checks can yield fewer than 96 structures on ocean-heavy or very mountainous source terrain.
- Live client visuals, collision feel and performance have not been manually playtested.
