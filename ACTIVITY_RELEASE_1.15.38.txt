Geometry and difficulty
- Removed the mirrored shaft-spanning beam pairs and their close secondary ribs.
- Each obstacle group independently chooses a broken bridge, stepped diagonal
  fragment, small layered rock island, or short hanging pillar.
- Positions, orientations, lengths and heights vary. Repeated group-origin
  columns are rejected instead of laying every layer out as another frame.
- A broad clear steering area accounts for body size and approach lag.
- Landing blockers are split into independently height-offset fragments spread
  through depth; they no longer form a dense aligned curtain above the lava.
- Lava targets are now 13x13, 11x11, 9x9, 7x7, and 5x5 (previously 11 to 3).
- Five levels still increase obstacle-group density and shrink the lava target.
- Normal falling/air steering, solid-impact failure, safe level handoffs, activity
  ownership, existing intro/skip flow, rewards and cleanup are unchanged.
- The reduced mineshaft mobs, Festival fixes, and lantern optimizations from the
  previous releases are retained.

Verification and limits
- Regression checks include 1,000 seeds for each of five levels and reject
  straight-down winning lava columns and shaft-spanning obstacle beams.
- Steering simulations cover both full input and 85% input response, checking
  actual swept body collision through the lava landing, not just waypoint reach.
- Human playtesting is still needed to judge subjective difficulty and readability.
- No new textures, models, audio files or runtime dependencies were added.
- All 79 activity GameTests passed in the final fresh-world run, including
  10,000 full/reduced-input steering-and-landing simulations and 5,000 generated
  courses checked for straight-down winning columns.
- Final test evidence: dropper38-tests-final.log.
- Live-client check cleared all five levels with normal falling and movement,
  including all four between-level teleport handoffs: dropper38-client.log.
- Rendered first/final-level screenshots were inspected; screenshot files:
  artwork/dropper38_level_1.png through dropper38_level_5.png.
- Production build and all six asset optimizer tests passed: release38-build.log.
- Package verification passed for 535 textures, 293 sound events, complete timing
  metadata, JSON/ZIP integrity and preserved recording durations/channels:
  release38-package.log.
- No development test/visual-check classes, test templates or transcription
  environments were found in the production JAR. Largest compressed entries are
  still existing activity music, approximately 1.7–2.2 MB each.
