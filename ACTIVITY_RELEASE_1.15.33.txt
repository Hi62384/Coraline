Coraline 1.15.33 — activity follow-up fixes

Music Festival
- Rotates Minecraft, Clark, Sweden, and Subwoofer Lullaby using installed vanilla
  sound resources. The previous visit's song is excluded from the next choice.
- Full track durations: 260.55, 201.60, 229.80, and 209.20 seconds respectively.
  The performance now lasts for its track instead of a fixed three minutes.
- Easier four-lane charts: at least 14 ticks between notes, 50-tick approach,
  and a five-tick timing window. No rapid-fire five-tick finale.
- Interactive harp accompaniment is derived from the recordings' onsets/pitches.
  Wrong/missed notes are detuned. This is an accompaniment arrangement, not
  isolated original stems: the original backing recording remains audible.
- Only 6.2 KB of chart metadata added; no Minecraft recordings copied into JAR.

Other current fixes
- Mansion: single-statement liar clues, each excluding only 2–3 of 12 doors;
  all six clues are necessary and together identify exactly one jackpot room.
  Wrong guesses reveal the jackpot room name, floor, wing, carpet and trim.
- Owner-only Skip dialogue button for Other Father activity introductions.
  Skipping advances the intro gate only; normal game/reward lifecycle is reused.
- Fortress: vanilla falling/steering, reachable late gap shifts, pool-floor
  landing accepted on all five levels instead of treated as obstacle collision.
- Mineshaft: local darkness, mainhand-only lantern, restored stair treads and
  landings, five-heart HUD above hotbar, amethyst geodes give amethyst shards.
  Nine depth-scaled enemy types include ranged shots, frost, pounces, charges,
  blink, non-destructive fuse attacks and heavy strikes. Fitted head-attached
  button eyes replace the old camera-facing icons and vanilla glowing eyes.
- End City: explicit white-light / gold-pad instructions, distinct yellow
  warning and purple active beam geometry shared with damage checks, three-heart
  HUD and brief recovering indicator after damage.
- Temple: one start-edge tile and one finish-edge tile, straight entry/exit,
  no side-sweeps along either edge or self-touching path branches.
