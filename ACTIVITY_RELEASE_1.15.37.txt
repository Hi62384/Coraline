Dropper correction
- Replaced the one-block random walk that preserved aligned openings with a
  normal-air-control sweep across the shaft and a reversal toward the lava.
- Seeded orientation, direction, bend timing and height variation change layouts.
- Higher levels also change the secondary steering axis.
- Beam openings account for player-body clearance and steering lag.
- A staggered broken furnace bridge blocks every straight vertical approach to
  the lava footprint. It is on the opposite side from the mid-fall steering route.
- Five levels, increasing obstacle density, and shrinking 11/9/7/5/3-block lava
  pools remain. No slow falling, forced horizontal propulsion or hidden gate tests.
- Normal-gravity live-client QA cleared all five levels and four teleport handoffs.
- New regression checks reject every straight-down winning column for 1,000 seeds
  across all five levels. Reachability checks follow actual swept collisions all
  the way to a safe lava landing, rather than just reaching intermediate waypoints.

Mineshaft population
- Enemy caps from uppermost to deepest floor: 2 / 3 / 4 / 6 (formerly 4 / 6 / 8 / 10).
- Spawn attempt intervals: 18 / 14 / 10 / 7 seconds (formerly 6 / 4.75 / 3.5 / 2.25).
- Ascending reduces an accumulated enemy crowd to the current floor's cap,
  removing the farthest surplus actors. Nearby remaining enemies retain behavior.
- Existing enemy variety, abilities, health, loot, maze size, lantern, map, reward
  ownership and exit flow are unchanged.

Scope and limitations
- This release addresses the aligned dropper and excessive mineshaft spawns.
- All 1.15.36 Festival and lantern performance changes are retained.
- Dropper client screenshots: artwork/dropper37_level_1.png through level_5.png.
- Difficulty was verified with a normal-input automated client; human playtesting
  is still the final judge of how challenging the courses feel.
- No new audio recordings, textures, or model assets were added.
