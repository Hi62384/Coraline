Coraline 1.15.47

Changes
- Secret dialogue can continue when the registered Mother is unloaded; bell-return dialogue no longer waits for proximity or teleports her to advance a line.
- Forbidden-area sightings interrupt current activity speech and start direct pursuit. Existing contact catch and four-second lost-sight memory remain. Nearby forbidden-item encounters outside forbidden areas retain their existing behavior.
- Ordinary-area caught dialogue completes without a second serious-scolding recording. Serious secret/item encounters retain serious-scolding recordings.
- activity_wait is limited to the first activity proposal in a fresh visit, not subsequent activity/reconciliation cycles.
- Portal placement moved ten blocks farther sideways: 55-63 blocks from the house origin, roughly thirty beyond the outer house footprint. Existing invalid portal positions are reselected by the existing lifecycle.
- Normal walking keeps useful paths instead of constantly rebuilding them, restores AI/gravity, checks movement every five ticks, and uses a 1.35 walking multiplier.
- New secret structures capped at 40 (10 per tier), with 64-block minimum center spacing instead of 28. Tier-distance progression preserved. Existing built structures are not deleted.

Verification
- All 65 Secrets/Exploration regression tests passed: mother-flow-final-tests.log.
- No live Minecraft client visual/audio/play-feel verification was performed.
- Release build and packaged asset checks are recorded in mother-flow-release.log and the handoff response.
