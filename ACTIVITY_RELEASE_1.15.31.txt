CORALINE 1.15.31 — ACTIVITY REVISIONS
Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge 47.4.x / Java 17

IMPLEMENTED
Shopping Mall: 24 categorized stores, eight offers per shop per visit, larger
whitelisted rotating catalogues, additional food vendors, rear food court, and
48 independently routed button-eyed shoppers across two floors. Paged shop UI.

Other Father: activity hosts use activity ownership instead of retired house
parent lifecycle/generation. Persistent visible hosts are created in loaded chunks.

Snowball Battle: recovery now respects the whole arena, upper areas and bridges.
Removed timed player relocation. Faster, gravity-compensated NPC throws and
stronger pressure on passive players. Winner's prize moved to a ground-level counter.

Treasure Hunt: default exit, shuffled landmark seal gardens, and generated clues
combining petal colours and lamp-count puzzles rather than naming destinations.

Mice Circus: bookie turns toward his backed mouse while watching the race.

Woodland Mansion: enclosed upper stair landing; persistent/recoverable witnesses,
including Peaceful-mode illagers; model-attached button eyes on allays and illagers.
Clues explicitly describe the jackpot room. Journal has rules and scrollable clues.

Signs: shared bright contrasting sign text, including Carnival sign writers.

Nether Fortress: five normal-gravity descents; responsive steering; 7/9/11/13/15
obstacle bands with narrowing openings and varied paths. Reward requires all five.

Mineshaft: seeded connected four-depth maze with 420 rooms, branching tunnels,
loops and walkable stair connections. No lit rail spine. 20-minute deadline and
five expedition hearts. Loaned Moonlit Lantern and mining pick are restored safely.
Return through the entry portal to keep the bag; timeout, defeat or interruption
loses it. Successful extraction queues rewards until after transport, so overflow
drops outside the erased activity. Deeper strata have better ores and more threats.
Button-eyed Stitched Miners pursue; Thread Skitters pounce; Shard Watchers telegraph
aimed strikes; Deep Stalkers blink. Actor count is capped; normal player health is
not used. Lanterns repel nearby threats intermittently, not continuously.
The existing finalized mining introduction is retained; its old automatic-payout
wording predates this extraction rule. The start title, timer and entrance sign
explicitly require returning to the portal to keep the haul.
Moonlit Lantern night mode now recognizes the fixed-night Other World dimensions.

End City: earned chorus teleports replace random/pity rolls. Traverse shuffled
glowing-rune circuits and return to the central gold pad to teleport. Telegraphs
warn of damaging energy beams. Five-minute deadline and three activity hearts;
timeout or lost hearts opens a safe failure exit without treasure.

Desert Temple: separated row sweeps and rejection of nonconsecutive touching tiles
prevent tangled gold clusters. Original five sizes and route-length progression
remain. 50,000 generated routes passed clean-path validation.

Six asset-optimizer tests passed. Final JAR validation passed for 535 textures,
242 sound events, JSON/ZIP integrity and full duration/channel preservation of
23 resampled recordings. No exhaustive human balance or multiplayer session run.

SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS
This release includes the preceding fixes already in 1.15.30 and the revisions
above. Automated server checks do not prove subjective balance or exhaustive
multiplayer/graphics compatibility. No new Blender or raster/audio assets were
needed; existing optimized assets are reused. Source audio/art masters are intact.
