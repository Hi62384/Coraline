Coraline 1.15.42 — journal, exploration and activity follow-ups

Implemented
- Journal: custom parchment panels and ink/gold controls; separate blackmail
  payment screen; physical interaction target; Move journal action; six highlighted
  bedroom placements; leaving the bedroom returns the carried display to its origin.
- Debug item: Debug: Mother Finds Journal (operator-only). Uses the existing
  discovery/consequence function, including removal of filed evidence.
- Activity exits: available during every active activity stage, plus a Leave
  activity pause-menu button for movement-locked games. Existing payout/abort rules
  remain in charge; quitting does not grant an automatic win. Intro UI/audio clears.
- Outdoor activity portal placement; removed repeated arrival teleport tether.
- Mother: inventory blackmail interruption; evidence checks at the portal;
  line-of-sight wandering catches 40 blocks outside the estate footprint;
  structure catches take priority over item catches. First-visit scene remains protected.
- Scolding: four-block movement allowance, explicit head/body facing, confiscation
  of genuine carried evidence and the owner-bound borrowed key. Filed evidence and
  ordinary inventory contents are not confiscated by this operation.
- Maximum suspicion: finishes the serious scolding and safely ends the visit,
  without offering another activity. Also repairs the old completed-voice dead end.
  This is a safe fallback, not an implemented boss/trap storyline.
- Structures: up to 48 per visit; estate-edge clearance, distance-based tiers,
  tier quotas, roof/canopy/window-box/trim variants. Terrain validation and the
  existing 96-block-per-tick construction budget remain. Existing structures are
  not destructively relocated during an occupied visit; revisit for a new layout.
- Spies: suspicion-scaled rat population, capped and recycled; non-player doll
  textures, sitting pose, support pedestal, inward facing and a 76-degree view
  cone. Brief watcher messages/glints identify detection without a large overlay.
- Festival: shorter opening note approach and spacing, actual detected source
  chords instead of invented repeated/rest-filling phrases; no autoplay backing
  notes; emerald donations replace button tokens; 180-second cap retained.

Verification
- First Secrets run: 40/40 passed.
- Updated Secrets retest: 43/43 passed (secrets-revision-retest.log).
- Runtime asset optimizer: six tests passed.
