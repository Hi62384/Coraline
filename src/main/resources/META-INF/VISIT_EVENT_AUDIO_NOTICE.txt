The Other Mother and Other Father visit-event recordings (exploration, study,
dinner, outings, game night, deliveries, parties, Mother Goes Out, Wybie's
adventure, theatre and mouse circus) are synthetic voice-cloned
audio generated locally with IndexTTS2 from the project's supplied Other Mother
Sample.wav and Other Father otherfathersample.wav references. They are not
original recordings by the reference speakers. No model weights are included.

Supplied original story recordings are retained separately, not presented as
newly synthesized performances. Subtitle drafts were transcribed locally and
source-checked; uncertain speech is explicitly marked rather than invented.
Automated checks are not a claim of human acting or listening review.
