Coraline 1.15.46 — Other Mother movement and dialogue pacing

Movement
- Let the normal head/look controller run while a scripted parent is walking.
- Synchronize parent movement every two ticks instead of the default three.
- Close-up conversations no longer force body yaw against the walking controller.
- Deduplicate conversation approach updates and use separate stop/resume distances to avoid shuffling around the player.
- Nearby NPC repositioning now requires a genuinely stalled approach; it no longer happens every two seconds while she is making progress.
- Portal/present waiting positions use a reachable stopping tolerance and a normal 1.2 walking multiplier, avoiding repeated tiny approach corrections.

Dialogue
- Cancelling an interrupted recording also releases its old welcomeAfter cooldown.
- Missing completion acknowledgement has a one-second grace rather than ten seconds.
- Inter-line padding reduced from ten ticks to two.
- Completed recordings can reconcile a lagging server tick clock using the recording's full real-time duration. Premature acknowledgements cannot skip that duration.
- Existing client-side consecutive-line queue remains intact to avoid overwriting an audible recording.
- No source audio edits, truncation or added media assets.
