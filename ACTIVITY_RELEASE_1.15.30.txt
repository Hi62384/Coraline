CORALINE 1.15.30 — SHOPPING MALL, SNOWBALL BATTLE, TREASURE HUNT
Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge 47.4.x / Java 17

SHOPPING MALL
Two-floor mall with 16 themed stores, a skylit atrium, contained fountain,
chandeliers, balconies, a rear staircase, kiosks, food-court seating,
merchandise displays, attendants and lightweight wandering shoppers.
Each visit starts with 1,000 activity-only Shopping Tokens. There is no timer.
Each store offers a fixed core product plus rotating stock, chosen once per visit.
The catalogue includes food, clothing, garden supplies, toys, furniture supplies,
books, tools, weapons, jewellery, premium equipment, treasure and potions.
Rare material products require prior ownership; story-critical items are excluded.
Prices range from 10 to 950 tokens. Expensive purchases require confirmation.
The browse/buy/cancel screen shows quantity, price, remaining stock and balance.
Purchases enter a persistent shopping bag and are delivered once after exit.
Full-inventory overflow drops at the destination. Unspent Shopping Tokens expire.
An exit is available after Father's existing introduction, even with no purchases.

SNOWBALL BATTLE
Winter arena with four snow forts, ice pond, bridge, raised overlooks, trees,
snowdrifts, flags, cabin-front stalls, seated spectators and a gold prize podium.
Owner versus six individually named and coloured NPC opponents, free-for-all.
Each contestant has five activity hit points. Normal player health is not used.
Father's existing introduction finishes before a protected three-second countdown.
The temporary pouch throws unlimited physical snowballs with a 12-tick cooldown.
NPCs have distinct ranges, movement and firing styles, fight each other, and
limit early concentration on the owner. Low-health retreat is distance-limited.
Occluded opponents are pursued around cover instead of being ignored indefinitely.
Eliminated NPCs leave combat. Last player standing claims one top-tier prize.
Loss opens the exit without a winner's reward. Late rallies and a five-minute
draw fail-safe prevent indefinite matches. Borrowed equipment, flight abilities
and remaining pre-existing effect durations are restored on completion/abort.

TREASURE HUNT
Five-minute exploration challenge starting after the existing Father introduction.
Six unique destinations per run: five collected clue seals, then a hidden vault.
Twelve authored landmarks include a crescent fountain, ruby bridge, lantern oak,
golden gazebo, clock tower, mushroom grove, greenhouse, ruined watchtower,
wishing well, chess garden, golden orchard and emerald-roofed cottage.
Connected paths, flower ribbons, hedges, arches, lit trees and underground vaults
frame the landmarks. Fountains/streams have physical containment.
Short authored clues use names, riddles, visual features, counting and relationships.
The first clue is explicit; the final clue identifies a sealed cellar.
No coordinates or destination waypoints. A temporary Treasure Note rereads the
current clue in a non-pausing parchment UI; a bossbar shows time and clue number.
Future clue objects cannot skip the chain. Decoy chests do not overwrite the clue.
Three possible vault destinations stay reward-locked until all five seals are found.
Claiming the treasure before timeout grants the existing top-tier reward once.
Timeout grants no jackpot, restores the borrowed item and opens a safe exit.

SHARED SYSTEMS AND ACCESS
All three extend the existing ActivityWorld / OtherMotherLoop lifecycle through
ExpeditionActivities. They reuse ownership, portals, finalized Father recordings,
protected structures, temporary item restoration, NPCs, rewards and safe cleanup.
They are in the normal activity rotation and have Creative debug items:
  Shopping Mall Activity Debug   (coraline:mall_activity_debug)
  Snowball Battle Activity Debug (coraline:snowball_activity_debug)
  Treasure Hunt Activity Debug   (coraline:treasure_activity_debug)
Debug entry retains the existing first-visit completion / bound-door requirements.
Helpers cannot spend the owner's budget, advance clues, fire owner snowballs or
claim the owner's prizes. Combat spectators are kept outside the playing area.
Existing project music is reused: ambient_feast, ambient_race and ambient_garden.
Purchase chimes, clue sounds, countdown cues, throws, impacts and result sounds
provide feedback without giant chat tutorials. No new audio masters were added.
