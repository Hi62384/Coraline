Other World exploration and spies - 1.15.43

Changes
- Structures can begin on copied terrain before the whole island finishes copying.
  Every footprint chunk is checked to prevent later terrain copying erasing it.
- Placement samples distance bands directly, reads actual earth under trees,
  accepts additional natural terrain materials, and falls back from unavailable
  tiers. Existing buildings, spacing, terrain checks and placement budgets remain.
  Retries run in existing visits; no fresh world is required.
- Rats ignore the house/garden and the 40-block buffer beyond it. Returning to
  this safe area clears partial sightings and pending reports.
- Nearby rats chirp. A watching rat stops, faces the player and chirps loudly.
  Reporting requires 80 uninterrupted ticks (four seconds at normal server speed)
  inside its forward field of view with clear sight. Lost sight resets progress.
  Spy-only movement speed is reduced to .18 with report multiplier 1.0.
- Doll detection hints use a chime and repeated glints, not screen text.
- Mother detection can interrupt ordinary dialogue away from the portal. Visible
  evidence/borrowed keys and restricted areas initiate the existing confrontation.
  Area dialogue keeps priority. The first-visit scene remains protected.
  Undecided blackmail offers are withdrawn without consuming the item; earned
  pending payments retain their receipt metadata.
- Active pursuit restores AI/gravity and dismounts old seats. After five seconds
  without progress it tries a validated nearby ground position, without moving
  the player or destroying blocks, then resumes navigation.
- An expired invitation remains in WINDOW with its portal and activity boss bar
  visible at 0:00. A persisted one-time random 1-10 minute delay starts the search.
  Late arrival cancels the search. Search state is not disclosed on the bar.
- Search speed is increased, with rotating patrol sectors and structure visits
  across the island. The target tracks the player only while visible; blocked
  sight leaves the last-known destination instead of tracking through walls.

Verification
- Optimized release build passed; six optimizer tests passed. Release verification
  validated 363 sound events, four charts, and exclusion of test code/audio masters.
  JAR size: 73,919,840 bytes, only 3,631 bytes larger than 1.15.42.
- All 51 Secrets server regression tests passed (secrets-late-arrival-tests.log).
- New checks cover tree-ground placement, building protection, partial island
  copying, rat safe boundary, four-second uninterrupted sight and forward vision,
  dialogue interruption away from portals, pursuit movement restoration, late
  attendance with a one-time delayed search, and wall occlusion during pursuit.
- Live client exploration, sound-volume tuning, and visual behavior were not
  tested. Structure placement still requires valid natural terrain; it does not
  overwrite buildings or force structures into oceans/cliffs.
- This release does not claim to resolve all issues in the earlier backlog.
