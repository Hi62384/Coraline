OTHER WORLD SECRETS / INVESTIGATION -- 1.15.39
Implementation and verification report

Status: integrated playable investigation system, with limitations listed in section 25.
This is not a claim that every requested storyline interaction has been completed.

1. SECRET DEFINITIONS
36 authored, registered, owner-bound evidence items. Each has a stable ID, title,
description, severity, discovery tier, attribute contribution and connections.
Duplicate evidence does not stack journal knowledge. Research is stored in the
existing per-story-owner VisitData ledger, not trusted client item data.

2. SIX ATTRIBUTES
Code Knowledge, Hidden Treasure View, Hidability, Pattern Recognition, Weakness,
Reward Manipulation. Each has six secrets worth 18 points, capped at 100.
Two secrets per access tier make progression attainable without circular locks.
Journal removal immediately recalculates totals. Effective advantages subtract
Mother's corresponding knowledge counter; rescued souls are a separate modifier.

3. THRESHOLDS
Code 33/66/100 unlocks three restricted tiers. Pattern 33 reveals the key drawer,
66 unlocks the structure map, 100 adds Mother's position. Treasure 100 reveals
and enables hidden caches. Hiding, rewards and weakness scale continuously.

4. INDIVIDUAL ABILITIES
Storage/passage/surveillance markers, temporary invisibility, false rat signals,
servant orders, Soulglass harvesting and prison rescue have runtime actions.
Vulnerability feedback indicates opportunities to escape. Combat modifiers are
implemented as damage hooks, but the current base Mother has no playable boss
fight and remains invulnerable; those hooks are not a completed combat feature.

5. LOOPS
Six explicit connected loops: Surveillance Hijack, Soul Liberation, Unravel
Illusions, Secret Delivery, Threadbreaker, Chorus of Servants. Completing hijack
and reveal also grants Unweave. Removing a required secret removes the ability.
Hijack uses an existing entity camera with a timed return, not remote possession.

6. JOURNAL
Native collection/details, search, multiple selection, graph, abilities and map
tabs. Deposit, deletion and blackmail require proximity to the physical journal.
Read-only field notes work away from it. Six hiding locations: writing desk,
closet shelf, bedside chest, dresser, loose floorboard, bookcase compartment.
The desk has a visible book; hidden locations do not display an exposed journal.
Destructive actions show a scrollable preview of exact lost knowledge/loops.

7. INSPECTIONS
Persistent suspicion-dependent inspection progress: approximately 50 minutes at
zero suspicion, 10 at half, 4 near maximum. Critical activity phases defer checks.
One hiding place is selected, with the conspicuous desk weighted more heavily.
Inspection currently resolves through the scheduler, not a physical animated
room-by-room search by Mother.

8. DISCOVERY CONSEQUENCES
Journal discovery exposes its specific contents permanently and applies configured
trust/suspicion consequences. Quiet undiscovered research alone has no penalty.
Visible forbidden interactions and carried evidence affect confrontations.
Deleting knowledge does not erase what Mother already learned.

9. BLACKMAIL
Selected journal entries are sacrificed server-side into a single-use owner-bound
token. Acceptance combines severity, trust and suspicion. Accepted offers allow
one of three material rewards, scaled through four severity tiers. Rejection
consumes the offer. Persisted nonces prevent copied/stale tokens being replayed.
Payout is at-most-once, not fully crash-recoverable: see section 25.

10. PERMANENT MOTHER KNOWLEDGE
Separate journal, exposed and suspected sets persist in the existing storyline
save. Ordinary gameplay cannot erase confirmed exposure. Privileged debug actions
can reset it intentionally. Normal journal snapshots do not expose Mother's ledger.

11. SIX COUNTERMEASURES
Code: faster, narrower, longer pin challenges.
Treasure: trapped cache probability.
Hiding: erodes sight-distance/report-speed benefits.
Pattern: more structure traps and greater trigger probability.
Weakness: erodes damage-hook advantages, pending actual boss integration.
Rewards: erodes gift improvements and can substitute low-value gifts.
Existing visit gift categories and presentation flow are reused.

12. SEARCH / CHASE
The existing five-minute activity window now expires. First three minutes are
on-time, final two are late. Missing it schedules a randomized search delay.
States track search, last sight/noise position, chase, confrontation and returning
home. Vision uses line of sight and hiding effects. Distant navigation uses local
waypoints. The porch bell recalls Mother at a relationship cost; leaving during
an active search is guarded through the existing exit flow.

13. DOLLS
Owner-scoped native display props watch a forward cone with line-of-sight checks.
Three continuous seconds cause a report; an obstruction or interruption resets
the accumulation. Reporting calls Mother and applies existing confrontation flow.

14. RATS
Existing MouseEntity gains a saved/synced spy role and button-eye rendering.
Rats watch, alert visibly, and physically travel to Mother before reporting.
They can be intercepted; false signals and servant commands alter behavior.
Visit-wide caps increase with suspicion instead of endlessly spawning near players.
Remote chunk unloading can pause a report journey.

15. NOISE / TRAPS
Noise carries source, time, range and actual position. Mother investigates that
position instead of obtaining continuous knowledge of the player's whereabouts.
Walls attenuate hearing. Structure plates and cache alarms use this same system.
Trap cooldowns prevent every tick from repeatedly punishing the player.

16. PROCEDURAL STRUCTURES
Twelve room themes across four tiers, with randomized visit positions/seeds:
cottage, pavilion, watchtower, doll workshop, library, clockwork storehouse,
thread observatory, mine, illusion chamber, Soulglass lab, soul prison, anchor.
Placement checks footprint, terrain slope, house exclusion and room separation.
Construction is a persistent queue capped at 96 blocks per tick. Cleanup records
original/placed blocks and avoids overwriting blocks changed since placement.
Themes use distinct native decorations within a common compact building envelope.

17. ACCESS / LOCKPICKING
Public plus three Code Knowledge tiers. Server-owned nonce sessions validate
owner, distance, expiry, live knowledge and input rate. Timed moving-cursor pins
open the actual doorway. Borrowed house keys and the attic use existing blocks.
Missing keys/open attic contribute suspicion; keys can be returned to their drawer.

18. CACHES
Knowledge-gated hidden cache nodes, visible markers and one-time reward claims.
Restricted room access is checked before interaction. Claim ledger is persisted
before rewards to prevent reconnect/replay duplication.

19. ORES
Protected, generated Other Iron, Other Diamond and Moonstone nodes require a
pickaxe and room access. They pay existing registered mod resources; arbitrary
Other World terrain remains protected.

20. SOULGLASS / RESCUE
Reuses the existing Soulglass item. Knowledge and room access gate harvesting.
Prison altars free visible captive Allays, persist rescue progress and remove the
captive with feedback. Each rescue modestly weakens pursuit/binding, capped at
15%, independently of journal totals. The requested larger trapped-player/boss/
button-eye ending progression is not present in the base project and is incomplete.

21. DEBUGGER
Operator-only registered item and native GUI. Eight relationship/counter controls,
numeric inputs, natural-counter reset, evidence/loop grants, journal edits,
awareness edits, inspection controls, search/chase/noise/bell-related testing,
spies/traps, world generation/teleport/unlock/reveal, forced blackmail outcomes.
Each request rechecks permissions. It does not have a separate convenience button
for every possible individual structure decoration or resource node.

22. MULTIPLAYER
Server derives owner from the sending player. Expiring sessions, physical journal
range, owner-bound evidence and single-use reward state prevent helper mutation.
Overflow rewards are reserved for their owner until actually picked up. Automated
ownership/replay tests ran; a real two-client multiplayer session did not.

25. LIMITATIONS / INCOMPLETE WORK
No playable hostile Mother boss, trapped-player threshold story or button-eye
ending integration: absent base storyline cannot be represented as complete by
adding damage hooks. Inspection selection is abstract rather than animated.
Building themes share a compact common shell, not elaborate bespoke interiors.
No full natural-world legitimate progression playthrough, real two-client test,
power-loss fault injection, or representative long-session performance profile.
Ledger-before-payout avoids duplication but an abrupt crash during delivery can
lose a payout; no transactional recovery outbox exists. Remote rats can pause in
unloaded chunks. Surveillance camera is an entity view, not full remote controls.
New Mother/Cat lines are text captions using existing presentation, not new voice
recordings. These limitations mean the entire 69-part request is not finished.
