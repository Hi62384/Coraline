1. Categories and rewards
The existing repeat-visit system now has Food, Treasure, Weapons, Armor, Tools,
Building Materials, Redstone & Machinery, Clothing, Music, and Mystery Gifts.
Food and Treasure retain their original generators, random-call order, quantities,
voice pools, platter/box interactions, and inventory-remainder behavior.

Weapons: iron swords, bows/crossbows with ammunition; later Needle Rapier,
Vine Sword, Thread Whip, Other Iron Sword, diamond sword, trident, Moonblade,
Button Mace, Midnight Wand, Spidervision Sword, and gated rare Perfect Sword.
Armor: iron/chainmail pieces; later Other Iron, Dreamweave, diamond, and rare
material-gated Moonstone, Other Diamond, or Perfect pieces. Not clothing duplicates.
Tools: iron mining tools, fishing rods, shears, brushes; later Other Iron/diamond
tools and gated rare Moonstone, Other Diamond, or Perfect tools.
Building: coherent cottage, quartz, garden, and cherry/color palettes, with
5–6 stacks; main materials come in 32/48/64-block bundles by progression.
Redstone: automation, railway, and secret-door bundles containing actual usable
redstone, hoppers, observers, pistons, repeaters, comparators, rails, minecarts,
droppers, switches, and sensors.
Clothing: actual wearable raincoat, pajamas, overalls, top hat; later Midnight Coat,
Star Shirt, Circus Jacket, Silent Slippers, Feather Boots, and Midnight Goggles.
Music: registered playable vanilla discs plus a jukebox or note blocks/bell;
Otherside is an occasional late gift. No fake unregistered music items.
Mystery: a surprise bundle drawn from those useful categories, sometimes with
food/emeralds or a rare late diamond extra; not a guaranteed jackpot.

2. Selection and balancing
Default configurable weights: Food 15, Treasure 15, Weapons 10, Armor 10,
Tools 10, Building 10, Redstone 10, Clothing 8, Music 7, Mystery 5.
Forge COMMON config: coraline-visit-gifts.toml. Zero disables a category; if all
weights are zero, Food/Treasure is the safe fallback. All categories have a valid
early pool. Visit tiers are before visit 4, visits 4–8, and visits 9 onward.
Rare Moonstone/Other Diamond/Perfect equipment also needs prior material/gear
via the existing inventory-based material/gear rule. Perfect food does not unlock Perfect equipment. Enchants are modest.
Mystery quality is 70% basic, 25% at most middle-tier, 5% current-tier, with the
same material gates. Reward generators reject empty/oversized/temporary loan items.

4. Registration and visit flow
New master paths: voicelines/othermother/loot/<category>/<before|after>/line_01..03.wav.
Runtime: assets/coraline/sounds/visit/other_mother/loot/<category>/<moment>/line_01..03.ogg.
Event IDs: other_mother_loot_<category>_<moment>_01..03.
The existing sounds/subtitle/timing tables are merged, not replaced; normal resource
processing rebuilds complete durations from packaged files.
Welcome by existing trust/suspicion state → saved BEFORE variant → physical gift →
saved AFTER variant → optional existing extra → existing tutorial/proposal/warning →
full five-minute relaxation → unchanged activity portal/lifecycle.
Category and both variants are committed once on entry. Reward ledger/prepared flag
and opening deadline survive save/load. New gifts use the existing ribboned GIFT
model, reveal for 1.5 seconds, then award. Mystery does not sync contents while closed.
Food/Treasure keep their old interactions. New-category overflow drops at the owner,
preserves ownership, and retains the ledger remainder if spawning the drop fails.
The Mother stands beside new gift displays rather than approaching through them.
Client gift transitions wait for actual previous sound completion; abort clears queues.

5. Multiplayer and recovery
Owner/generation/door/range checks are reused. Helpers cannot open, claim, or pick
up owner-tagged gift overflow, even when vanilla's item-owner timeout would relax.
Saved category/voices/contents do not reroll on reconnect. A current voice may replay
after reconnect, but no new gift is generated or duplicated. Existing abort/reset
policy remains; missing audio does not create an endless reward timer.
Activity Food/Treasure prize themes are stored separately for the eight new categories
so adding visit categories does not unintentionally alter activity reward balancing.

6. Requested activity follow-ups
Fortress: safe next-level boarding position, teleport-acknowledgment grace period,
cleared crossing state, and actual solid support checks instead of stale onGround
alone. Normal falling/steering remain; lava clears the level, not an obstacle loss.
Mineshaft: descending shafts moved beside rooms; landing connectors preserve both
stair treads/headroom and the lower floor's corridor junction, including a sole exit.
Enclosed side bridges and capped shaft ends prevent walking past the last tread
into the void. Failure causes are also retained for diagnosing interrupted runs.
Map stair markers reflect the new shaft locations; mine size is unchanged.
Moonlit Lantern: removed the two-updates-per-second throttle. Held sources use
fractional positions, light recalculates each movement tick, occlusion flood fields
are cached separately, and only changed render sections are rebuilt. The old moving
invisible-block light layer is removed; legacy portable blocks clean themselves up.
Held lighting is render-only and no longer modifies block light or mob-spawn rules;
placed lanterns remain normal light-emitting blocks. Custom mine lamp repulsion remains.
End City: arena-wide shuffled tile positions plus fresh beam angles each cue;
client rendering and damage use matching rotated geometry with precise SAT collision.
Existing increasing level speeds and visible warning interval are retained.
Festival: complete note-block transcriptions of Minecraft, Clark, Sweden, and
Subwoofer Lullaby, choosing a different track on the next visit. No original recording
plays in the background. Quiet synthesized chord notes fill melody gaps; prompted
melody notes play on input, and wrong/missed melody notes are detuned. 293–318 playable
notes and 585–915 chord attacks per song. Scroll approach decreases smoothly from
30 to 16 ticks and spacing tightens from roughly 12 to 6 ticks, without simultaneous
four-button bursts. Natural rests/fades remain instead of inserting unrelated notes.
Basic Pitch ONNX transcription is development-only; model/venv/cache are not shipped.
Primary tool documentation: https://github.com/spotify/basic-pitch
Three tiny harp registers derived from the installed vanilla sample total ~24 KB.
These are simplified note-block arrangements, not exact studio-recording recreations.
