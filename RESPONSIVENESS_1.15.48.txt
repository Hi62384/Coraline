Coraline 1.15.48

Observed cause
The running 1.15.47 client log recorded repeated integrated-server stalls,
including 9-19 seconds behind. Six of eight live server-thread samples were
inside RealmCopy.advance -> ServerChunkCache.getChunkFuture, which managed-
blocks on the server thread while chunks load. This stalls entity movement,
inventory interactions and tick-based dialogue even in singleplayer.

Changes
- Request chunk futures through the non-blocking main-thread entry point.
  Poll completion on later ticks; do not access Minecraft worlds off-thread.
- Hold temporary full-chunk tickets until copy completion; release on completion,
  failure, cancellation, or stale-job replacement. Reduce background concurrency
  from four source/target pairs to two. Preserve the 500-block map.
- Apply actual playback completion clock correction to all Other Mother lines,
  rather than only reconciliation proposals. Preserve recordings in full.
- Door-bound mice retain running paths instead of rebuilding them periodically,
  with bounded retries when a path ends.
- Portals use their own clearance footprint, search a wider outdoor side area,
  and fall back to Father's study rather than ending the visit.
- Unowned decorative dolls in the Other World use varied existing parent textures,
  not the local player's skin. Owned personal dolls keep their owner's skin.
- Correct the spy doll display's half-block vertical offset. Repair existing
  placements once queued structure construction finishes; do not destroy furniture.
